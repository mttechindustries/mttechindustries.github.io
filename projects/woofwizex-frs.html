WoofWizeX-FRS

Companion-animal sensing research and validation platform

WoofWizeX-FRS explores privacy-aware, contactless sensing workflows for companion-animal monitoring using ambient-signal observations and controlled data provenance. The current public status is research and validation—not a clinical, diagnostic, or field-accuracy claim.
